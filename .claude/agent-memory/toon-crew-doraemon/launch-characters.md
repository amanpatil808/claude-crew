The owner keeps the current crew characters and names for the launch (2026-11-01). His view: the sprites are loose, off-model takes and only the names match the originals. He plans to change the names a little in future.

**Why:** Dekisugi's README research flagged trademark and copyright risk from well-known studio characters. The owner accepted the risk for now.

**How to apply:** don't re-raise it on every release. Mention it once when the repo is about to go public, and again if a takedown or complaint comes in. Any renaming waits for the owner to ask.
